Richmond College Model United Nations (RichMUN) 🌍🏛️Welcome to the official repository/documentation for RichMUN—the Model United Nations club and annual flagship conference of Richmond College, Galle, Sri Lanka.📖 OverviewRichMUN is dedicated to fostering diplomacy, critical thinking, public speaking, and global awareness among students. Rooted in Richmond College’s long-standing tradition of excellence and leadership, the club prepares delegates to analyze complex international issues, represent diverse foreign policies, and collaborate toward actionable resolutions.Beyond internal training and inter-school delegations, RichMUN hosts its annual conference, bringing together young delegates from across Sri Lanka to engage in high-level debate across specialized UN and crisis committees.🎯 Core ObjectivesDiplomacy & Negotiation: Train students in parliamentary procedure, consensus-building, and resolution drafting.Global Literacy: Encourage deep research into geopolitics, international law, human rights, economics, and sustainable development.Oratory & Leadership: Build confidence in formal speeches, moderated caucuses, and impromptu crisis responses.Conference Excellence: Organize an inclusive, competitive, and intellectually stimulating annual MUN conference in Galle.🏛️ Club ActivitiesWeekly Training Sessions: Workshops on Rules of Procedure (RoP), position paper writing, lobbying, and public speaking.Mock Simulations: Practice committee sessions designed to prepare first-time and experienced delegates alike.Inter-School Delegations: Representing Richmond College at national and international Model UN conferences.RichMUN Annual Conference: Organizing committees, study guides, executive board recruitment, and delegate logistics for our flagship event.🌐 Typical Conference CommitteesRichMUN conferences feature a diverse lineup of committees tailored for beginner, intermediate, and
